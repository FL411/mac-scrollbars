# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Mac Scrollbars changes the visual appearance of scrollbars on ordinary web pages.

## Data Handling

- The extension does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data.
- The extension does not read page content, form values, browsing history, passwords, or payment information.
- The extension does not use analytics, advertising, or external servers.
- Appearance settings, including any sites the user chooses to exclude, are stored with the browser's `storage.sync` API. The browser may sync those settings through the user's browser account according to the browser's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- `storage`: saves appearance preferences.
- Access to ordinary web pages: applies the selected scrollbar appearance. No page content is collected or transmitted.

For support, use the contact details displayed on the extension store listing.
